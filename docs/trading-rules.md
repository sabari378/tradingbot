# Initial XAUUSD Trading Rules

Nirv initially evaluates M5, M15, and H1 context. A potential long requires higher-timeframe bias, approach to sell-side liquidity, a confirmed sell-side sweep, bullish MSS, displacement, bullish FVG retracement, minimum 1.5 RR, acceptable spread, no high-impact news blackout, risk approval, and explicit user approval. Shorts invert the liquidity and structure conditions.

These are configurable starting rules, not financial advice. Tick volume is not centralized exchange volume. No market data provider means no trade candidate.
